# Incident lite

## Workflow

1. Record observed symptoms, start time if known, affected users, and evidence sources.
2. Separate confirmed impact from suspected scope. Identify recent changes and available recovery options.
3. Compare rollback or mitigation against data compatibility and operational risk; obtain required authorization before production actions.
4. Record actions actually taken, recovery checks, unresolved questions, and next owner/task.

## Completion

A brief evidence-based incident note with impact, timeline, mitigation, and next check. No invented timestamps or blame.
